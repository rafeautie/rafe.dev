# Story notes

The story's memory. Rewritten with every chapter; keep it under about 2,000
words, folding older detail into "The story so far".

## Characters

- **Ellis Ward**: courier, late thirties. Took the job to clear a debt. Grieving
  his younger brother Danny, who died a year ago; hears Danny's voice in his
  head, teasing. Dry, tired, kinder than he lets on. Laughs rarely.
- **Pell**: the Kestrel's computer. Short, dry, literal sentences. Pauses when
  deciding whether to be offended. Named by the previous pilot, who never said
  why.
- **The previous pilot**: a woman who flew the Kestrel before Ellis. Learned the
  cello badly. Scratched a small bird beside the airlock (not yet confirmed).
- **The clerk at Ceres freight**: unnamed, unimpressed.

## The ship and the case

- **Kestrel**: twenty-year-old courier ship, one bunk, one galley, many warning
  lights, a reputation for getting there. Registration KS-7741.
- **The case**: dark blue, lunch-tin sized, warm. Seal blinks green and "knows"
  Ellis. Rules: don't open it, don't let it get cold, don't let anyone else hold
  it for more than a minute or "it tells Thule". Kept in a locker by the bunk.
  Its light may have blinked faster when the signal came.

## Places

- **Ceres**: departure. Freight office on the outer ring.
- **Thule**: research station at the edge of the solar system. Destination,
  about 290 days out.

## The story so far

1. Departure, More or Less: Ellis leaves Ceres with the case. First night, a
   faint signal from ahead: seven pulses, a gap, three pulses, every 91 minutes
   exactly. Matches nothing on record.

## Open threads

- What is the signal, and why does it come from ahead, along their route?
- What is in the case, and why does it react?
- Who was the previous pilot, and why "Pell"?
- Ellis's debt: who he owes, and how much it mattered.
- Danny: what happened, and what Ellis hasn't faced.

## Where it's heading (loose plan, next few weeks)

- Ellis and Pell try to decode the signal; small discoveries, no answers yet.
- First human contact on the radio: a mining crew or a lonely relay operator.
- The case shows another reaction that can't be explained away.
- A first real problem with the ship that Ellis has to fix himself.
- Hints about the previous pilot found aboard (the cello, a log, the bird).
